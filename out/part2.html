Nvidia. Friday's issue covered Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia's CEO. What changed over the weekend: calls for him to come back grew. CNBC reports that a petition asking for his return has passed 170,000 signatures, and Fortune put it at about 175,000 on Monday. CNBC says the backers include well-known tech founders and investment firms, and notes that the petition site's labels for current and former Nvidia employees are self-reported and not verified.

The board is moving the other way for now. CNBC says it is still weighing inside and outside candidates, is picking a search firm, and has not formally engaged anyone. Nvidia reports earnings on Tuesday, November 17, and that call is the first time interim CEO Colette Kress speaks to investors in the role.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOU

A petition is public sentiment you can count. It shows how loyal a brand's fans and partners can be to one person, and how little that loyalty guarantees. For marketers, this is a live case of a brand and its leader being tied together, and a ready example when a team talks through how it would handle a brand crisis. That is our read, not a forecast.

CLIP AND TRY TODAY

Make a three-column list: who wants Huang back, who wants an outside CEO, and who is waiting for facts. Write one sentence for each group on what they would want to hear from Nvidia on Tuesday. It is a quick stakeholder-map exercise you can reuse for any brand.
