Tip: turn customer feedback into a table

Paste a batch of customer comments into any AI chat tool and ask: "For each comment, list the main topic, the tone and any request. Show me the list first, then group the topics and count how often each one comes up."

How you might use it: it turns scattered feedback into something you can paste into any spreadsheet. Asking to see the list first lets you catch misreadings before you rely on the counts. Remove names and contact details before you paste.

Anthropic's prompting guide has a section on controlling the format of a response. The idea below is our own application of it: instead of hoping for a good layout, hand the AI the exact blanks to fill, and you get the same shape every time.

WEAK

Compare these two competitor product pages.

STRONG. COPY AND PASTE THIS

Compare these two product pages for me.

Page A: [paste page A]

Page B: [paste page B]

For each page, fill in this template and keep the labels exactly as written:

Product name:

Company:

Top 3 benefits they claim:

Price stated:

Main call to action:

One question a customer might still have:

If something is not on the page, write "not stated". Do not guess.

Then add one line saying which page makes the stronger case to our audience and why. About our audience: [one sentence about who you sell to].

Why the strong one works

  • Same shape every time. Both pages come back in identical blocks, so you can scan across them.

  • Nothing gets skipped. Each blank is a required field, so the AI cannot quietly leave out the price.

  • Gaps show up. "Not stated" tells you what the page left out, instead of a made-up answer.

  • Easy to reuse. Keep the template and swap in new pages, or paste results into a spreadsheet.

CLIP AND TRY TODAY. 5 MINUTES

Take two real competitor pages, or two versions of your own campaign landing page. Run the prompt, then check each filled-in field against the original text. Note one field the AI got wrong or marked "not stated." About 5 minutes.

Sources: Anthropic prompting best practices (the part on controlling response format). The template layout and the "not stated" rule are our own adaptation.
